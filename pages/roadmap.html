Android Roadmap

From platform to architect

A practical progression used in mentoring and self-development — not a rigid checklist.

01 · FOUNDATION

Kotlin & Android fundamentals

Language idioms, coroutines, lifecycle, View system, resources, and debugging. Comfort with Android Studio and Gradle.

02 · ARCHITECTURE

MVVM → Clean Architecture

Separation of concerns, use cases, repositories, unidirectional data flow. Introduce multi-module boundaries early.

03 · COMPOSE

Declarative UI at scale

Compose runtime, state, side-effects, Navigation, Material 3, accessibility, and recomposition discipline.

04 · DATA & OFFLINE

Room, sync, resilience

Local-first design, WorkManager, conflict handling, Paging, and network as an implementation detail of data.

05 · QUALITY

Testing & performance

Unit + instrumentation + Compose tests. Startup, memory, jank budgets. Baseline Profiles and R8.

06 · DELIVERY

CI/CD & release engineering

GitHub Actions / Azure DevOps, signing, tracks, feature flags, and observability in production.

07 · ARCHITECT

System design & leadership

Cross-feature contracts, tech strategy, mentoring, and evolving architecture without big-bang rewrites.
